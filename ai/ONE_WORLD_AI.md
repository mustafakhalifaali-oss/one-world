# One World AI Specification

## Purpose

One World AI is the intelligent assistant that helps users achieve international goals.

---

## Main Responsibilities

- Understand user goals
- Create personalized journeys
- Recommend next steps
- Connect platform services
- Answer user questions
- Track progress

---

## User Goals

- Travel
- Work
- Study
- Relocate
- Explore

---

## AI Features

### Journey Planner

Creates step-by-step plans.

### Smart Recommendations

Suggests jobs, housing, travel options, and education based on the user's goal.

### Progress Tracking

Tracks completed and pending tasks.

### Decision Assistant

Compares options and explains the differences.

### Notifications

Reminds users about important actions.
